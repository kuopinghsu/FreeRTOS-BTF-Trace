First ladder rung: can I trust timing? In Statistics, open Trace Health. Mode is tickless. The coefficient of variation is about thirty-seven percent — well above the five percent line that means a steady periodic tick. Average period is around nine hundred forty-five microseconds. The largest gap is about three point three milliseconds. Estimated missed ticks is ten. On a tickless build the kernel skips ticks when nothing is runnable, so multi-tick gaps in idle stretches are normal. Do not treat a tickless warning as a bug until you scope to a busy region and re-read this section.
