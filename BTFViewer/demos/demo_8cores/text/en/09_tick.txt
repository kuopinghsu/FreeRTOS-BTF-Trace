Step 1: check whether the timing data is reliable.

In Statistics, open Trace Health. This trace uses tickless mode. The coefficient of variation is about 37%, which is much higher than the 5% reference for a stable periodic tick. The average period is about 945 microseconds. The largest gap is about 3.3 milliseconds, and the estimated number of missed ticks is 10.

In tickless mode, the kernel skips ticks when no task is ready to run. Long gaps during idle time can therefore be normal. Limit the scope to a busy region and check Trace Health again before treating this warning as a defect.
