Start with system health. In Statistics, open Trace Health. Mode is tickless. The coefficient of variation is about thirty-seven percent — well above the five percent line that means a steady periodic tick. Average period is around nine hundred forty-five microseconds. The largest gap is about three point three milliseconds. Estimated missed ticks is ten. On a tickless build the kernel skips ticks when nothing is runnable, so multi-tick gaps in idle stretches are normal. Before treating missed ticks as a defect, place cursors on a busy window and re-read this section. For this demo, treat tickless idle as a lower priority: do not let idle gaps spoil every latency number until we scope the view.
