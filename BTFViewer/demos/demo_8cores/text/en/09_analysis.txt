Open Analysis from the activity rail.

The findings summarize the most useful investigation leads for the current scope. In this trace, they include balanced core utilization, high CPU consumers, long execution slices, off-CPU delay candidates, suspected priority inversion, excessive migration, tick-health warnings, synchronization bounces, and execution-time spikes.

Each finding points to measured evidence and the Statistics section to inspect next. Confidence also matters: measured utilization and execution times have high confidence, while findings based on mixed causes or heuristic thresholds need more verification.

Use a finding to decide where to look. Then confirm the evidence in Statistics and on the timeline before drawing a conclusion.
