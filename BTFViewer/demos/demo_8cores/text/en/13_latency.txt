Now look at latency in this cursor window. Blocking Time is how long a task stayed off CPU until it ran again. High two-sixty-eight waits up to about fifty-three milliseconds here. Low and Med wait about thirty-five to forty milliseconds. Ignore Runner — that task sleeps between test phases on purpose. Blocking is not the same as dispatch latency: dispatch asks, after create or resume, how long until the first switch-in. Preemption Chain names who preempted whom. Priority Inheritance also belongs in this window. Low is boosted by mutex inherit — the kernel doing its job; look for red stripes under that task. A Low, Med, High pattern on task PS two-twenty-eight is the classic three-task inversion and deserves a design review, not only a tuning change.
