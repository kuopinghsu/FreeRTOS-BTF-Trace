Fifth rung: check latency, inside the inversion window again. Blocking Time is how long a task stayed off CPU until it ran again. task High two-sixty-eight waits up to about fifty-three milliseconds here. Low and Medium wait about thirty-five to forty milliseconds. Ignore Runner — that task sleeps between test phases on purpose. Blocking is not true end-to-end response time. Dispatch asks, after create or resume, how long until the first switch-in. Preemption Chain names who preempted whom. Priority Inheritance also belongs here. Low is boosted by mutex inherit — the kernel doing its job; look for red stripes under that task. A Low, Medium, High pattern on task PS two-twenty-eight is the classic three-task inversion and deserves a design review.
