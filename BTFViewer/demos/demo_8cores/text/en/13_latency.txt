Step 5: check latency inside the priority-inversion window.

Blocking Time measures how long a task stays off the CPU before it runs again. In this window, task High[268] waits up to about 53 milliseconds. Low and Medium wait about 35 to 40 milliseconds. You can ignore Runner because it sleeps between test phases by design.

Blocking Time is not the same as end-to-end response time. Dispatch measures the delay from task creation or resume to its first switch-in. Preemption Chain shows which task preempted another task. Priority Inheritance shows temporary priority changes caused by a mutex. Look for the red boost bar below Low.

Task PS[228] shows the classic Low, Medium, and High priority-inversion pattern. This pattern needs further design review.
