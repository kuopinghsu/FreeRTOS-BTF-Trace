That completes the BTFViewer investigation workflow.

Start with the full timeline and Statistics. Read the verdict and Analysis Findings, then inspect trace health, load balance, CPU execution, latency, migrations, synchronization, and configuration evidence as needed. When a trace contains several phases, use cursors to keep the analysis scope focused.

Use AI after the evidence is visible. Follow its jump:TIME links, check the same events in Statistics and on the timeline, and treat unsupported or out-of-scope claims as unverified.

The workflow stays the same from beginning to end: find the evidence, narrow the scope, investigate the cause, verify it against the trace, test a change, and compare a new measurement.
