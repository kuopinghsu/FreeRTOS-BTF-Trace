Step 9: use AI after Statistics and the timeline have established the evidence.

The assistant works from BTFViewer's findings, statistics, timeline queries, and other evidence available for the current scope. The scope is important: evidence outside the selected cursor range should not be used to support a scoped conclusion.

Open Settings, then AI, enable the assistant, choose the model, enter any required credentials, test the connection, and save. Local models can also be used when configured.

Choose the context level that fits the investigation. Compact reduces context, Balanced is the normal default, and Full Evidence provides more detail for difficult cases.

The AI tab follows six stages: Triage, Scope, Investigate, Verify, Experiment, and Compare. Start Investigation provides the guided entry point. AI can organize and test explanations, but Statistics and the timeline remain the measurement reference.
