The toolbar is mostly icons — hover any icon for its name. You can switch layout between Horizontal and Vertical. Zoom in, zoom one-to-one, or Fit the whole trace. Task view versus Core view. Load turns on the CPU utilisation strip under the timeline. Heatmap and Chord show migrations between cores.

Now look at Analysis on the toolbar — it opens a short triage card of findings. Compare sits next to it when two traces are open. Settings is on the far right.

On the right-hand panel: Statistics, Marks, Find, Legend, and AI. We work in Statistics first, then finish on AI.
