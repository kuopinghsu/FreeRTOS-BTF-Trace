Most toolbar buttons use icons. Move the pointer over an icon to see its name. You can switch between Horizontal and Vertical layouts. Use Zoom In, 1:1, or Fit to adjust the timeline. You can also switch between Task View and Core View.

Load shows CPU utilization below the timeline. Heatmap and Chord show task migrations within the visible time range. To inspect the full trace, click Fit before opening these migration views.

Analysis opens a short triage card of findings. Compare becomes available when at least two traces are open. Settings is at the far right.

The right panel contains Statistics, Marks, Find, Legend, and AI. Start with Statistics. Use AI only after the statistics and timeline show the same evidence.
