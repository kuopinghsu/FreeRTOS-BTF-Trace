The toolbar is mostly icons — hover any icon for its name. You can switch layout between Horizontal and Vertical. Zoom in, zoom one-to-one, or Fit the whole trace. Task view versus Core view. Load turns on the CPU utilisation strip under the timeline. Heatmap and Chord show migrations between cores; they show migration data for the range you can see on screen. To see migrations for the full span, Fit first, then open the migration views.

Now look at Analysis on the toolbar — it opens a short triage card of findings. Compare is available when two or more traces are open. Settings is on the far right.

On the right-hand panel: Statistics, Marks, Find, Legend, and AI. We work in Statistics first. AI comes last, after the timeline agrees.
