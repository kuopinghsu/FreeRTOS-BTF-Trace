Second rung: are the cores sharing the work? Open Core Utilisation. Cores sit roughly fifty-seven to seventy-five percent busy. Load Balance Score is ninety-five percent, and the spread — sigma — is about six percent. So imbalance is not the main story. Do not stop here. Then check Core Time Breakdown, Concurrent Core Active, and Kernel Switch Overhead. A scheduler can keep every core equally busy by bouncing work between them. Good balance and expensive migration often appear together.
