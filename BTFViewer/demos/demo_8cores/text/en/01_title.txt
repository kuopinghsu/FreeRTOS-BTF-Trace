Welcome to BTF Viewer — an interactive app for Free-RTOS context-switch traces in Best Trace Format. The desktop and web apps share the same analysis. Today we use the desktop app and the frozen eight-core demo sample.

This capture lasts two point four two one seconds, on eight cores, with a hundred and sixty-three tasks. It was built by stitching several stress tests together, so warnings are expected. We will inspect the timeline, read Statistics from the top down, confirm each issue on the canvas, then try the AI assistant on Google Gemini.
