Step 4: separate CPU usage from long execution slices.

Top Tasks by CPU shows several CS worker tasks with similar CPU shares, around fourteen percent of active CPU time. High CPU share does not mean that the same task has the longest single execution.

Execution Time Per Slice shows the largest observed user-task slice on NC[118], at about 110.2 milliseconds. This is an observed maximum, not a proven WCET. The report also flags tasks whose Max value is much larger than their typical execution, including Runner[1] and several CS tasks.

Use the Max value to locate the slice on the timeline. Then check preemption and synchronization evidence before deciding why the slice is long.
