Step 3: find the tasks that use the most CPU and the longest execution slices.

Open Top Tasks, then Execution Time Per Slice. Task CS[27] uses about 15% of the CPU time. Its longest slice is about 3.5 milliseconds. Sort by Max and click that value to move the timeline to the slice.

Do not check only the average. A 3 to 4 millisecond slice is long compared with a 1 millisecond tick. You can set a deadline for each task under Settings, Display, Analysis thresholds. The values use nanoseconds. Before deciding that the task itself is slow, also check Preemption Chain and Mutex.
