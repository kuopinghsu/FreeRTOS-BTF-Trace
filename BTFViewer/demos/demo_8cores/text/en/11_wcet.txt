Next, attribute CPU time to tasks. Open Top Tasks, then Execution Time Per Slice. task CS twenty-seven is about fifteen percent of CPU, and its worst single slice is around three point five milliseconds. Sort by Max and click that Max value — the timeline jumps to that slice. Three to four milliseconds is long next to a one millisecond tick: one slice can span several tick periods and delay everything behind it. Later you can set per-task deadlines under Settings, Display, Analysis thresholds — those values are in nanoseconds. Always check Preemption Chain and Mutex before blaming the task’s own work.
