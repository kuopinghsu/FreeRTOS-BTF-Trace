Step 1: check trace timing health.

Trace Health reports TICKLESS mode with an interval coefficient of variation of about 34 percent. The average tick period is about 948 microseconds, the largest gap is about 2.5 milliseconds, and the report estimates five missed ticks.

This is a warning, but tickless operation can naturally create irregular tick intervals. Check the large gaps against the timeline and the active workload. If necessary, narrow the scope to a busy region before deciding whether the timing behavior is actually abnormal.
