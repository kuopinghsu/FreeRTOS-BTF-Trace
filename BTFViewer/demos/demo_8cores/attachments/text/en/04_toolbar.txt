Most toolbar controls use icons. Hover over an icon to see its name. You can switch between Horizontal and Vertical layouts, zoom the timeline, use one-to-one view, or click Fit to show the visible trace clearly. Task View groups activity by task, while Core View groups it by CPU core. Load adds CPU utilization below the timeline.

The activity rail on the left contains Migration Heatmap, Analysis, Investigation Notebook, Compare, Save Image, Help, and Settings. Analysis opens the current findings. Investigation Notebook is a per-trace document that records why you reached a conclusion: typed bookmarks, an evidence chain, and a conclusion. Migration Heatmap opens the migration view. Compare becomes available when at least two traces are open.

The panel dock on the right contains Statistics, Marks, Find, Legend, and AI. We begin with Statistics. Use AI after the measurements and timeline have established the evidence.
