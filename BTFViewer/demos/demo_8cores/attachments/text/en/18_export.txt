Step 8: save the evidence and prepare to verify a change.

The Statistics HTML report starts with the overall verdict, key indicators, Analysis Findings, and links to the detailed evidence sections. Tables can also be saved as CSV from the exported HTML report. The report also carries the Investigation Notebook: its bookmarks, evidence chain, and conclusion. The same notebook travels inside the portable workspace file, so the reasoning stays with the trace.

After changing the firmware, capture a new trace and compare the same workload and scope. Keep the candidate and baseline aligned. A What-if estimate can suggest an experiment, but only a new measured trace can show whether the change actually improved the behavior.
