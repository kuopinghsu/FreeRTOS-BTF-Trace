Step 3: check how evenly the cores share the workload.

Across the full trace, core utilization ranges from about 55 to 72 percent. The Load Balance Score is 95 percent, with a standard deviation of about 5.8 percent. The report therefore considers the workload reasonably balanced.

All eight cores are active at the same time for about 33 percent of the trace. Kernel switch overhead averages about 2.9 percent of core time.

Good load balance does not mean low scheduling cost. Check migration activity next, because frequent movement between cores can keep utilization even while adding scheduling and cache-related cost.
